ABOUT THE FINISHED-ROD COLLECTION

Before you choose.

Hand-wrapped designs, available rods, and the details to look for.

Are the rods already made?

Yes. The available collection focuses on finished rods that we have already customized with hand-wrapped designs. Choose from the rods listed, each with its own photos, price, and specifications.

What kinds of designs do you wrap?

Designs include American flags, birds, and decorative patterns. Browse the current collection to see which finished designs are available.

Can I see the wrap up close?

Each listing supports multiple photos of the actual rod, including close-ups of the artwork. Open a product photo for a larger view. If you need another angle, ask about that rod.

Are these rods mainly for salmon and plunking?

Salmon and plunking are part of CaCaCa’s focus. Check the individual rod’s specifications for its intended use and builder-supplied recommendations.

What line, reel, or sinker should I use?

Use the recommendations supplied for the specific rod. If a detail is missing, ask the builder before choosing your setup.

What happens when a rod sells?

It leaves the available collection. Sold rods may remain in the wrap gallery so you can see past work; a gallery listing does not mean that rod is still for sale.

Do you take special requests?

Our focus is the finished rods in the shop. You can send a special request for the builder to review, but availability and acceptance are not guaranteed.

Where do you ship?

Shipping is limited to the United States. Shipping rates and delivery estimates will be confirmed before online checkout opens.

What is the return policy?

Visit the returns page for the current published information.
